Academic life

Studying at AMS.

Students learn best when they feel confident, safe and engaged at school.

At AMS, our experienced team of staff and teachers is committed to an environment that nurtures academic achievement and challenges every student to reach their full potential.

01

Pre-K · KindergartenPreschool

Your child's experience in these years orbits around essential skills in pre-literacy, curiosity, discovery and cooperation. With an Early Childhood program designed by AMS senior educators, students are supported socially, emotionally and academically.

Every student is seen as a unique individual with incredible potential, and we journey alongside them each day in this era of monumental growth.

02

ElementaryElementary School

Al-Alamiya Elementary School makes learning relevant and meaningful through an inquiry-based, integrated curriculum that focuses on the child as a learner.

We recognise that children come to us as distinct individuals, with unique experiences, backgrounds, abilities and interests.

03

MiddleMiddle School

Middle school is an essential time, as our children begin to become young adults. We carefully blend caring relationships with responsibility and reflection.

Children learn academic skills and also the interpersonal skills of teamwork, personal responsibility, academic integrity and social justice. Building character and awareness makes them masters of their own learning — ready for their last four years at AMS.

04

American DiplomaHigh School

We train students to actively question ideas and assumptions rather than accept them without doubt. Students understand, analyse and evaluate ideas, arguments and findings — the entire picture. Our final goal is to build independent thinkers.

Students show their learning in many ways, from project-based learning to online and paper exams. Every student has the devices they need at their fingertips, so technology is part of their learning every day.

Home & school

A partnership with every family.

We believe in a strong home–school relationship built on partnership and effective two-way communication with parents. Our doors, phone lines and inboxes are always open, and we welcome every chance to strengthen this bond.
