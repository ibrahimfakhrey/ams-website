School campus

Built to spark curiosity.

A unique location in Benha, designed to engage your child's curiosity and encourage a love of learning.

The building

Modern design, modern learning.

The building follows the latest thinking in school design and technology. Every classroom is air-conditioned and equipped with a smartboard, and Wi-Fi covers the whole campus.

Safety first

Your child's safety, taken seriously.

  • Fireproofed cables and conduits throughout the building.
  • Wall sealing built to contain a fire for two hours.
  • An extensive smoke-detection system.
  • A clear evacuation policy, ready for the unlikely event of an incident.
Early years

Learning through play.

Our Pre-K and Kindergarten building has interactive spaces inside and play areas outside — designed on purpose so young children learn through play.

Here they learn to take appropriate risks, cooperate, work as a team and develop fine and gross motor skills. Special rooms for pretend play, art, cooking and music help them widen their interests and discover new talents.
