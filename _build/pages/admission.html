Application steps

Four steps to joining us.

Apply online

Fill in the online application form. A tutorial video below shows you how.

Visit the school

Our admission team arranges a visit for you to:

  • Pay the admission fee.
  • Sign the application.
  • Submit the required documents.

Placement test

Your child takes a placement test for their grade level.

Meet the principal

Parents meet the principal to talk about our commitment to international education and our Egyptian heritage.

Please fill in the registration form and submit it with the required documents. Submitting an application does not guarantee admission.

Admission priorities

Who we admit first.

  1. Students who have siblings already enrolled at AMS.
  2. Students transferring from schools with similar programs, especially those following the American Diploma.
  3. Students with strong academic records, depending on available places and the date of application.
