About us

A broad, balanced education — with roots and wings.

Al-Alamiya Modern School, the first international school in Benha, offers a broad and balanced approach to education, enriched by a variety of co-curricular activities that equip all students with essential skills across every subject.

AMS follows an American curriculum, so students reach the high standards that lead to the American Diploma — and go on to Egyptian universities and renowned universities worldwide.

Our vision

AMS strives to create a collaborative community that maintains exceptional educational standards, values the diversity of cultures, prioritizes well-being, and upholds the essence of the Egyptian identity.

Our mission

To achieve our vision, the school works to:

  1. Develop students' critical thinking and leadership skills, and build 21st-century skills.
  2. Foster respect, understanding and appreciation for cultural diversity among all stakeholders.
  3. Create a safe, supportive environment.
  4. Instill loyalty and pride in Egyptian heritage, values and culture.
