About Cognia

An outside promise of quality.

Cognia is a non-profit, non-governmental organization that accredits primary and secondary schools throughout the United States and internationally.

Founded in 2006, Cognia represents more than 36,000 institutions, with a total of 25 million students and 5 million educators in about 80 countries.

AMS is accredited by Cognia (formerly AdvancED) from pre-kindergarten through twelfth grade. Enrolling your child in an accredited private school helps ensure that your son or daughter receives an outstanding education.

During the 2019 – 2020 school year, AMS hosted a Cognia external team for accreditation — and AMS is proud of the team's findings.

Standards of quality

At AMS, we meet the Cognia standards of quality.

I

The school demonstrates a clear purpose and direction.

II

The school shows strong governance and leadership.

III

The school provides proper teaching and assessment for learning.

IV

The school offers various resources and support systems.

V

The school uses results for continual improvement.
