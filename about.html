About us

Benha's first international school.

A dynamic, student-centered school that meets 21st-century global goals while celebrating Egyptian heritage.

Who we are

An American curriculum, an Egyptian heart.

Al-Alamiya Modern School, the first international school in Benha, offers a broad and balanced approach to education, enriched by a variety of co-curricular activities that equip all students with essential skills across all subject areas.

AMS follows an American curriculum, ensuring students achieve high standards that lead to the prestigious American Diploma. As a result, AMS students are accepted not only at every Egyptian university but also at many renowned universities worldwide.

Our school promotes a dynamic, student-centered learning environment that aligns with 21st-century global goals while celebrating Egyptian heritage. AMS is distinguished by high achievement, engaged learners, mutual respect, shared knowledge, collaborative learning, continuous improvement and tangible results.

Vision

AMS strives to create a collaborative community that maintains exceptional educational standards, values the diversity of cultures, prioritizes well-being, and upholds the essence of the Egyptian identity.

Mission

In order to achieve the AMS vision, the school works to:

  1. Develop students' critical thinking and leadership skills, and involve 21st-century skills.
  2. Foster an atmosphere of respect, understanding and appreciation for cultural diversity, considering all the stakeholders.
  3. Create a safe, supportive environment.
  4. Instill a sense of loyalty and pride in the Egyptian heritage, values and culture.
